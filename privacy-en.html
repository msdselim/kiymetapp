Kıymet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-26 · Developer: Muhammed Selim Doğan · kiymetapp@gmail.com

This is a draft prepared with a code-consistency check, not a substitute for legal counsel. The owner should have this reviewed by a qualified lawyer before publishing, especially the international-transfer and consumer-rights sections.

1. Introduction

Kıymet ("the app") is committed to protecting the privacy of your personal financial information. The app supports multiple profiles (up to 5 on the same device); this policy applies identically to every profile.

2. Data Storage — Local-First Approach

Your financial data is stored on your device and is not sent to any server by default.

  • Encryption: AES-256, key protected by Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
  • Multiple profiles: Each profile's data is stored separately, under the same encryption.
  • Local backup: Optional encrypted .kbk file, password-protected by you. If the password is lost, nobody — including the developer — can open it.

3. Microphone — Voice Entry

Speech recognition runs on-device. Raw audio is never written to disk or sent to a server; only the recognized text is used to fill transaction fields, and that text is stored encrypted. Declining the permission only disables voice entry.

4. Camera — Receipt / Screenshot Scanning (OCR)

Receipt and portfolio-screenshot scanning is processed on-device (Google ML Kit — no image is uploaded). The raw photo is never saved or transmitted; only the extracted text, after your confirmation, is stored encrypted.

5. Google Sign-In and Cloud Backup

If you sign in with Google, your name and email are used for authentication. The optional encrypted cloud backup uses a separate password you choose, encrypted before it ever leaves your device. This password is kept only in your device's secure storage and never sent to the server — no one, including the developer, can decrypt it without that password. Your Google account is not linked to your financial data.

7. Payment and Subscription

Premium subscription is processed via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. Payment details are handled by Google Play; the app/developer never sees them.

8. Analytics, Diagnostics and Advertising

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash diagnostics, no personal/financial data, can be turned off in Settings.
  • Firebase Analytics: Usage measurement, no personal/financial data, can be turned off in Settings.
  • Google AdMob: Ads shown to non-premium users (never loaded for premium). May collect an advertising identifier and device info. ATT prompt shown on iOS; AD_ID permission required on Android.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from that age group.

10. Data Transfer and International Servers

Google and RevenueCat servers may be outside Turkey. Your financial data is excluded from this transfer — it stays on-device.

11. GDPR — Rights of EU/EEA Users

  • Access
  • Erasure — "Clear All Data" and "Delete My Account and Cloud Data"
  • Portability — encrypted .kbk backup
  • Objection — no automated profiling

12. Changes

You will be notified in-app of material changes.
